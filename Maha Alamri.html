Credit Risk Intelligence

project

Loan outcome prediction, compared across TabPFN, Random Forest and Logistic Regression. The first job was removing every field that only exists after a loan has already been repaid or written off. Those fields make a model look brilliant in the notebook and useless on the day it meets a new applicant.

Preprocessing lives in one ColumnTransformer: imputation, scaling and one-hot encoding. Class imbalance is handled with random oversampling, hyperparameters with GridSearchCV, and the decision threshold is tuned for macro F1 instead of sitting at the default 0.5.

Flood Risk Intelligence

project

Flood events cluster in space and time. If neighbouring locations land on both sides of a random train/test split, the model can memorise the neighbourhood and the score overstates what it would do somewhere new. So evaluation held out by region and by time period.

SMOTE was ruled out for the same reason. It creates synthetic positives by interpolating between existing ones, which here means inventing places and moments that never existed.

Guardian · road + driver, watched together

Guardian Safety Car

A safety co-pilot that watches two things at once: the road ahead and the person driving. Perception measures how fast each obstacle is closing in. A decision layer then escalates one step at a time, from a quiet notice to a warning to assisted braking, and only takes over when the driver hasn't responded. It is designed to back the driver up, not replace them.

Turnaround Guardian

projectaviation safety

During a turnaround, a fuel truck, a baggage loader, a catering truck and a pushback tug all work around one aircraft against the clock. The danger is timing: a vehicle can be in exactly the right place at the wrong moment.

The goal: reason about ramp risk through the aircraft's state. The same spot is safe while the aircraft is parked and dangerous once the beacon is on or an engine starts. Turnaround Guardian combines anomaly detection and predictive monitoring to warn before the state changes, and a human supervisor stays in charge of every call.
